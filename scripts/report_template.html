Does buying the dip pay?

3/6-month return is how the stock you bought did over the next 3 or 6 months, weighted by dollars, so every buy gets the same amount of time. Timing edge is what you have today minus what you'd have if the same dollars had bought the same stock at its average price over the previous 3 months.

Trades & method

Trade log

Every filled order from Webull's order history. These are the exact cash flows used in every shadow comparison.

Method & data quality

  • Trades: pulled read-only from Webull's order history. Filled orders only, dated in US Eastern time.
  • Prices: Yahoo Finance daily closes, with splits applied to older trades. Dividends are modelled as reinvested on the ex-date, matching Webull's automatic reinvestment.
  • Shadow portfolio: each day's net buys go into the ticker at that day's adjusted close. Sells come out of it. If a ticker started trading after your first trade, money waits in cash until its first trading day.
  • IRR: the yearly return on your invested money, from those exact cash flows. Time-weighted return: daily returns (close ÷ (previous close + that day's net buys)) multiplied together. For a single ticker, this is simply its total return.
